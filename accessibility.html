Accessibility Help

For help using kanencoffee.com or help.kanencoffee.com, or to report an accessibility problem, contact our team.

service@kanencoffee.com (510) 859-4425

Tell us what happened

To help us understand the problem, please include:

  • The page address and what you were trying to do.
  • What prevented you from completing the task.
  • How you would prefer us to contact you.

You can also share your browser or assistive technology, but this is optional. You do not need to share a diagnosis or medical information. Please do not send passwords or payment card details.

Shopping and repair assistance

Contact us with product questions, repair questions, or for help with appointment options. If a page, video, review, checkout, or booking tool is difficult to use, tell us what information or assistance you need.

You can also visit our troubleshooting guides or contact page.
